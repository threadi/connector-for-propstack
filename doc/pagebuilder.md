# Page Builders

We support a wide range of page builders that make it very easy to set up the presentation of Propstack properties.

## Notes

If a specific page builder isn't listed here, [feel free to contact us](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/connector-for-propstack/). Alternatively, you can always use [shortcodes](shortcodes_de.md), though they offer less flexibility.

Support for each page builder is identical in the content provided; the actual design often depends on the capabilities of the specific page builder being used.

Most page builders are supported exclusively in the premium version of the plugin. You can find information about that [here](https://laolaweb.com/plugins/propstack-wordpress-plugin/propstack-plugin-pro-bestellen/).

## The Page Builders

### Block Editor

* 6 blocks and 2 templates (Archive & Single) in the free plugin
* 1 additional block in the premium plugin

### Avada

* 7 widgets and 2 templates (Archive & Single) in the premium plugin

### Bricks

* 7 widgets in the premium plugin

### Brizy

* 7 widgets and 2 templates (Archive & Single) in the premium plugin

### Elementor

* 7 widgets and 2 templates (Archive & Single) in the premium plugin

### WpBakery

* 7 widgets in the premium plugin
